官方介绍 （Modrinth · 联网获取）

The Block Box is exactly what the name says - a box full of blocks!

Here you'll find a variety of block sets, decorations, light sources and furniture. Ever wanted to build an opulent mansion with gold? How about an industrial complex with iron and copper? Or maybe you just want to sit down in a chair and watch the sky lanterns fly? This mod has got you covered!

The Block Box aims to extend the block palette of Minecraft wherever it makes sense, adding blocks for themes and use cases that aren't well-covered by existing blocks.

Also, the mod does not generate anything in your world - you can just drop it in at any time!

Take a look around and see if anything fits your next build!

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Can I use this mod into my modpack?

A. Yes! Feel free to feature The Block Box in your modpack, and customize it as you see fit for the experience you're crafting. Just keep in mind:

Make sure to credit, and don't claim The Block Box as your own creation; Always read the changelogs! Minor and major versions can sometimes have breaking changes, which will be explained there.

Q. Which versions of Minecraft will you support?

A. Currently, The Block Box is only available for Minecraft 1.21.1, but I might backport it to 1.20.1 once I have time. No promises!

Q. Do you have a Patreon, or similar means of supporting your work?

A. Not currently, but thanks for your consideration! Although I am currently unemployed, the money I earn from my projects is enough to sustain me in the meantime.

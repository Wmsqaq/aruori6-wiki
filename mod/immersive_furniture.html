Immersive Furniture

Is your Minecraft build missing just the right piece of furniture? But none of your 749 installed furniture mods have what you need? Look no further! Immersive Furniture is the perfect solution for you. Create custom and fancy furniture, deco, and objects in-game or choose from a vast online library. Share your creations with the world and download furniture made by others.

Over 3500 pieces of furniture are already available in the online library, with more being added every day. Big thanks to the amazing community contributing their creations!

Features

  • Online library to share and download furniture
  • In-game editor to create your own furniture
  • Supports particles, sounds, sitting and sleeping
  • Automatic material-based texturing
  • Toggleable states (e.g. open/closed) and lighting

A simple yet powerful in-game editor allows you to create good-looking furniture with ease.

Craft the Artisan's Workstation to get started!

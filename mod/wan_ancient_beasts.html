Version 1.4.0 (16/01/2026) – 黄金三镖客更新（The Good, the Bad & the Ugly Update）

剧透预警 # **新增远古生物** ## 1) Charger（冲锋兽） * 中性生物，可以通过它的蛋复活 * 它时不时会对地坐下偷个懒 * Raider（掠袭者）的天敌：一见面就会攻击它们 * 只有在被先攻击、或玩家手持**甜菜根**时才会攻击玩家 * 如果喂它甜菜根，它会变得**极度狂暴**，见谁攻谁并造成双倍伤害（该效果在首次命中后结束） * 可以像驯马一样驯服它，但难度大得多： * 当你从它身上摔下来时，它会攻击你一次 * 喂食**甜浆果**能让驯服更容易 * 驯服后可以装上鞍并骑乘 **能力** * 按下 **C** → 头槌攻击 * 按下 **空格键** → 冲锋攻击（双倍伤害 + 击退，沿直线冲锋，需要充能） * 可以用**发光浆果**繁殖 --- ## 2) Raider（掠袭者） * 敌对生物，可以通过它的蛋复活 * 攻击性极强的掠食者，会攻击一切生物（与 Eater（吞噬者）相似） * 会被吞噬者吓到，但吞噬者不会攻击它 * 给它喂食**远古肉（Ancient Meat）**就能让它变为中性 * 变为中性后，只有被激怒才会攻击 * 群体猎手：攻击其中一只会激怒整群 * 冲锋兽的天敌 **战利品机制** * 它击杀生物时会掉落更多战利品（基础为**抢夺 I**） * 喂食新的**瓶子草（Pitcher Plant）**可以将其提升至**抢夺 IV** * 用头部攻击时，有几率掉落一根它的角 * 可以用任意种类的肉繁殖 * 会作为灾厄村民部队出现在它们的专属结构中，也会参与袭击 --- ## 3) Snatcher（夺物兽） * 中性生物，可以通过它的蛋复活 * 体型小、速度快，凭借带钩的爪子可以攀爬各种表面 * 偶尔会从玩家背包和箱子里偷走食物或物品 * 想拿回被偷的东西，打它一下就行： * 如果不及时打它，它会把物品吞下肚，物品就永远消失了 **驯服** * 让它从你的背包里偷走它最爱的食物——**Nasty Stew（恶心炖汤）** * 驯服后可以给它装上**木桶** **能力** * 看着它按下 **C** → 它会爬上你的肩膀 * 再按一次 **C** → 把它扔出去 * 如果扔在箱子或木桶附近，它会偷走战利品并带回到主人身边 --- # **新增生物** ## 1) Tamer（驯兽师） * 新的灾厄村民变种 * 出现在袭击中以及掠袭者的结构里 * 通常会骑着掠袭者随队行动 * 手持一把剑 --- # **新增物品** ## 1) Charger Armor（冲锋兽铠甲） * 可以在地牢中找到（通常出现在冲锋兽结构里） * 可以染色 * 效果与**金马铠**完全相同 --- ## 2) Raider Horns（掠袭者之角） 由掠袭者掉落，可以合成出三种变体： ### • The Fearsome Horn（骇人之角） * 用 **Eater Teeth（吞噬者之牙）**合成 * 对 20 格范围内的所有生物施加**恐惧（Fear）**效果，持续 5 秒 * 冷却时间 10 秒 ### • The Shrieking Horn（尖啸之角） * 用**回响碎片**合成 * 发出强大的爆震，对 20 格范围内造成沉重伤害 * 冷却时间 10 秒 ### • The Spectral Horn（幽冥之角） * 用**幻翼膜**合成 * 立即击杀 20 格范围内的所有**幻翼**和**恼鬼** * 冷却时间 10 秒 --- ## 3) Nasty Stew（恶心炖汤） * 用于驯服夺物兽 --- # **新增嗅探兽植物** 嗅探兽现在可以挖出以下新种子： ## 1) Rafflesia（大王花） * 装饰性花卉 * 效果类似**孢子花**，但长在地面上 ## 2) Sundew（茅膏菜） * 装饰性植物 * 会吸引飞虫（类似萤火虫灌木丛） ## 3) Equisetum（木贼） * 装饰性史前植物 ## 4) Moonflower（月光花） * 装饰性花卉 * 表现类似**粉红色花簇** * 可以放置在泥土类方块上 ## 2) The Training Outpost（训练前哨站） * 出现在**平原**生物群系 ## 3) The Primal Log（太古巨木） * 出现在**原始（Old Growth）**类生物群系

Version 1.3.0 (10/08/2025) - 冲浪更新（Surf Update）

剧透预警 # **新增远古生物**  ## Surfer（冲浪者） - 蛋可以在 Dead Coral（死亡珊瑚）中找到 - 它是和平生物   - 幼年时可以喂它任意种类的珊瑚：如果喂食大量同种珊瑚（32 到 64 个），它会根据珊瑚种类改变颜色；如果喂的是多种颜色（同样在该数量范围内），则不会变色  - 既能在水里游，也能在陆上走，不过在陆地上会慢得多 - 在陆地上它会惬意地晒太阳：如果你靠近得太快，它会受惊逃跑；如果你潜行靠近，它就会安静不动；只要你友好而缓慢地接近，就能与它互动并尝试驯服它  - 驯服它需要在它放松时喂它珊瑚扇（珊瑚种类取决于冲浪者的颜色；如果它还是默认颜色，那么任何珊瑚都可以）  - 驯服后可以装鞍骑乘；它在陆地上行动缓慢，但在水中和水下速度很快（比船稍快一点）  - 冲浪者能储存大量氧气，骑乘时按下 C 键，它可以为你恢复氧气，最多 3 次  - 在水中靠近水面时按下空格键，它会跃出水面并冲出很远的距离（需要 10 秒充能）  - 可以用海泡菜繁殖  - 它还可以装备“冲浪者铠甲（Surfer Armor）”：这会提供一层额外保护，但会降低它的速度（变得和船一样快）  # **新增物品** ## Surfer Armor（冲浪者铠甲） - 可以用鳞甲（Turtle Scute）合成 - 效果和狼铠完全一样，会替它承受一切伤害，改为消耗自身耐久 - 无法染色 - 可以用鳞甲修复（和狼铠一样） # **新增结构** ## Dead Coral（死亡珊瑚） - 出现在石岸生物群系

Version 1.2.0 (02/05/2025) - 古生物更新（Paleo Update）

剧透预警 # **新增生物** ## 1) Paleontologist（古生物学家） - 属于村民群体的和平生物 - 在本模组的所有结构中都有极低概率生成 - 在发掘场中更常见 - 可以与你交易本模组中的物品 # **新增方块** ## 1) Crusher Spikes（碾碎者尖刺） - 碾碎者尖刺现在既可以放在地面上，也可以像钟乳石那样放在方块底部 - 它们极其致命，小心各种可能的陷阱 ## 2) Quicksand（流沙） - 一种新方块，和细雪非常相似，找化石的时候最好离它远点 ## 3) Fossils（化石） - 一种新的装饰方块，只在发掘场出现，也可以通过与古生物学家交易获得 # **新增结构** ## 1) The Dig Site（发掘场） - 可在所有生物群系中找到 - 依据生物群系不同，里面有大量可疑的沙子、沙砾、红沙或冰，让你能找到所有远古巨兽的蛋（包括嗅探兽） ## 2) 恶地神庙（Mesa Temple）、冰刺城堡（Ice Spike Castle）和沙漠遗迹（Desert Ruins）的新变种 - 来体验经典 WAB 结构的新变种吧，还带了一些别出心裁的小变化 # **新附魔：远古棍棒（Ancient Club）** ## 1) Blood Thirst（嗜血） - 让你在处于任意药水效果时造成更多伤害 - 与 Life Steal（生命窃取）不兼容 ## 2) Life Steal（生命窃取） - 让你每次命中敌人时吸收半颗心 - 与嗜血（Blood Thirst）不兼容 ## 3) Hunter's Mark（猎人印记） - 对动物和远古巨兽造成更多伤害 # **新特性** ## 1) 悬挂滑翔翼（Hang Glider）染色自定义 - 把一份染料和一个悬挂滑翔翼放进工作台，就能改变羽毛的颜色 ## 2) 复制陶片 - 把一片陶片和一块红砖放进工作台，就能得到 2 片相同的陶片

Version 1.4.0 (16/01/2026) – The Good, the Bad & the Ugly Update

Spoiler # **NEW ANCIENT CREATURES** ## 1) Charger * Neutral Mob that can be brought back to life with its eggs * From time to time it will sit down and loaf * Natural enemy of Raiders: attacks them on sight * Attacks the player only if attacked first or if the player is holding a **Beetroot** * If fed a Beetroot it becomes **over-aggressive**, attacking anyone on sight and dealing double damage (effect ends after the first hit) * Can be tamed like a horse, but it’s much harder: * When you fall from it, it will attack you once * Feeding **Sweet Berries** makes taming easier * Once tamed, it can be equipped with a saddle and ridden **Abilities** * Press **C** → Headbutt attack * Press **Spacebar** → Charge attack (double damage + knockback, straight line, requires recharge) * Can be bred with **Glow Berries** --- ## 2) Raider * Hostile Mob that can be brought back to life with its eggs * Highly aggressive predator that attacks every mob (similar to the Eater) * Scared by the Eater, but the Eater does not attack it * Can be made neutral by giving it **Ancient Meat** * Once neutral, it will attack only if provoked * Pack hunter: attacking one will aggro the entire pack * Natural enemy of Chargers **Looting Mechanic** * When it kills a mob, it drops more loot (base **Looting I**) * Can be increased up to **Looting IV** by feeding it the new **Pitcher Plant** * When attacking with its head, it has a chance to drop one of its horns * Can be bred with any type of meat * Found as Illager troops in their own structures and during Raids --- ## 3) Snatcher * Neutral Mob that can be brought back to life with its eggs * Small and fast, can climb surfaces thanks to hooked claws * Occasionally steals food or items from player inventories and chests * To retrieve stolen items, hit it once: * If not hit in time, it will devour the item and it will be lost forever **Taming** * Make it steal its favorite food, the **Nasty Stew**, from your inventory * Once tamed, it can be equipped with a **Barrel** **Abilities** * Press **C** while looking at it → it climbs onto your shoulder * Press **C** again → throw it * If thrown near a chest or barrel, it will steal loot and bring it back to the owner --- # **NEW MOBS** ## 1) Tamer * New Illager variant * Found during Raids and in Raider structures * Usually rides a Raider troop * Equipped with a sword --- # **NEW ITEMS** ## 1) Charger Armor * Found in dungeons (commonly in Charger structures) * Can be dyed * Works exactly like **Golden Horse Armor** --- ## 2) Raider Horns Dropped by Raiders and craftable into three variants: ### • The Fearsome Horn * Crafted with **Eater Teeth** * Applies the **Fear** effect to all mobs in a 20-block radius for 5 seconds * 10s cooldown ### • The Shrieking Horn * Crafted with **Echo Shards** * Emits a powerful blast dealing heavy damage in a 20-block radius * 10s cooldown ### • The Spectral Horn * Crafted with **Phantom Membrane** * Instantly kills all **Phantoms** and **Vexes** in a 20-block radius * 10s cooldown --- ## 3) Nasty Stew * Used to tame the Snatcher --- # **NEW SNIFFER PLANTS** The Sniffer can now find the following new seeds: ## 1) Rafflesia * Decorative flower * Works like a **Spore Blossom**, but on the ground ## 2) Sundew * Decorative plant * Attracts flies (similar to a firefly bush) ## 3) Equisetum * Decorative prehistoric plant ## 4) Moonflower * Decorative flower * Behaves like **Pink Petals** * Can be placed on earth-like blocks ## 5) Ginkgo Trees * New saplings that grow into 1x1 or 2x2 trees * Includes a full **Ginkgo Wood Set** (planks, doors, etc.) ## 6) Olden Moss * New moss variant --- # **NEW BLOCKS & MECHANICS** ## 1) Paleo Table * Crafted with **4 planks** and **2 pottery sherds** * Used to: * Duplicate pottery sherds * Transform beast eggs into decorative blocks * Acts as the job site block for the **Paleontologist** profession --- ## 2) Paleontologist (Villager Profession) * Created using the Paleo Table * Can be leveled up to unlock new trades --- ## 3) Wandering Paleontologist * Found exclusively in **Dig Sites** * Has unique trades * Rework of the former Paleontologist --- ## 4) New Banner Patterns * New patterns usable on shields --- # **NEW STRUCTURES** ## 1) The Ruined Castle * Found in **Plains** biomes ## 2) The Training Outpost * Found in **Plains** biomes ## 3) The Primal Log * Found in **Old Growth** biomes

Version 1.3.0 (10/08/2025) - Surf Update

Spoiler # **NEW ANCIENT CREATURE**  ## Surfer - Eggs found in the Dead Coral - It’s a peaceful Mob   - It can be fed any type of coral and if fed a big quantity of the same type (from 32 to 64) as a baby, it will change colour depending on the type of coral; if fed with mutiple colours (in that range of 32 to 64) it will not change colour  - Can both travel in water and on land, though on land it will be significantly slower - When on land it will relax basking in the sun: if you go near it too fast it will get scared and it will try to run away, while if you crouch it will remain still; if you approach it nicely and slow you will be able to interact with it and attempt taming it  - To tame it you'll have to feed it Coral fan (the type of coral depends on the colour of the Surfer, if it has its base color then any type of coral is good) while it's relaxed  - Once tamed it can be equipped with a saddle and you'll be able to ride it; it can travel slowly on land, and fast in and under water (slightly faster than a boat)  - The Surfer is able to store big quantities of oxygen and if you press c while riding it, it will be able to restore your oxygen up to 3 times  - If you press space bar near the surface while in the water it will jump out and will cover a great distance (it takes 10 second to recharge)  - It can be bred with sea pickles  - It can be also equipped with the “Surfer Armor”: this will add a layer of protection but will reduce its speed (now beign as fast as a boat)  # **NEW ITEM** ## Surfer Armor - It can be crafted with Turtle scutes - It works exactly like dog armor and will tank any damage, consuming its durability instead - Cannot be dyed - Can be Repared (like-dog armor) with turtle scute # **NEW STRUCTURE** ## Dead Coral - Can be found in the Stony Shore biome

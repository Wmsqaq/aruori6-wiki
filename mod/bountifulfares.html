Kaleidoscope Bountiful Fares

A compatibility and content addon that bridges Bountiful Fares (丰饶食记) with Kaleidoscope Cookery (森罗物语：厨房). It brings Bountiful Fares' tea and fruits into the Kaleidoscope cooking system, adds new recipes across Kaleidoscope machines, and introduces a ceramic kitchen knife.

Features

Placeable Tea

  • Bountiful Fares tea can now be placed in the world, compatible with Kaleidoscope Cookery's tableware system.

Fruit Platters

  • Golden Apple Platter
  • Rotten Apple Platter
  • Lemon Platter
  • Orange Platter
  • Plum Platter

Recipe Integration (all added to existing Kaleidoscope machines — no new workblocks)

  • Millstone — new grinding recipes
  • Fermentation Jar — new pickling recipes (requires Kaleidoscope: National Flavor / 森罗物语：国味)
  • Pot / Wok — new dish recipes

Tool

  • Ceramic Kitchen Knife — a new knife for ingredient preparation and cooking.

Dependencies

Kaleidoscope: National Flavor (森罗物语：国味) ⚙️ Optional Fermentation Jar — pickling recipes only activate with this installed

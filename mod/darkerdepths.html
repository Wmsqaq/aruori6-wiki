Darker Depths 是一个地下世界扩展模组。

目前 Darker Depths 新增了 3 种生物群系：

Glowshroom Forest（荧光蘑菇森林）

剧透 荧光蘑菇洞窟是你找到大型荧光蘑菇、荧光蘑菇怪物、glowgrime 以及 Glowshroom Cap（荧光蘑菇帽）的地方。 Glowshroom Cap 戴上后耐久有限，会发出光亮，还会给佩戴者提供急迫 I。

Sandy Catacombs（黄沙地下墓穴）

剧透 Sandy Catacombs 是一片荒凉的废土。石化的树根从洞顶垂下，aridrock（干旱岩）与巨石散布在洞穴内部。 这里栖息着一类没有灵魂的新型生物。它们在地下墓穴中游荡，渴望与下一个受害者绑定在一起。 Void Soul（虚空之魂）生物被击杀后并不会死去，而是化为 Void Souls。这颗宝珠无法被伤害，但 60 秒后就会消散。手持玻璃瓶右键点击，就能把 Void Souls 收进瓶罐里；手持琥珀右键点击则能阻止它们消散。 这里还有一座新结构，名字就叫 Catacombs（地下墓穴），你会在其中遇到 Body Snatchers（夺躯者）、Void Soul Knights（虚空之魂骑士）、Tombs（坟墓）、Paranoia（妄想怪）以及 Forsaken Bronze（遗弃青铜）。 受 Void Souls 之力的诅咒，这种 Forsaken Bronze 能赋予非同寻常的能力。这种金属可用于合成若干实用物品，包括 Death Anchor（死亡之锚）、Stiletto（短剑）和 Tombs（坟墓）。

Molten Cavern（熔洞）

剧透 Molten Cavern 是一个酷热难当的生物群系，这里的深板岩已变成 Darkslate（暗板岩），熔岩以分层水池的形式分布。间歇泉、Amber Clusters（琥珀晶簇）以及 Dead Living Crystals（死寂水晶）都能在这里找到。 给 Dead Living Crystals 一颗钻石，就能将它们唤醒。从那时起，它们会开始逐渐恢复生机。完全长成后，它们会变成 Living Crystal（活性水晶），可以蔓延到附近的钻石矿上，也可能——只要给它足够的时间——爱上西瓜的味道。 当 Living Crystal 对西瓜产生了渴望，Crystal Melon（水晶西瓜）便会诞生。把一个西瓜放在活性水晶旁边，它会先变成一颗石瓜，随后水晶在内部不断生长，最终破壳而出，化作 Crystal Melon。 把 Crystal Melon 放在副手中使用，它会为任何工具超级充能：使其无法损耗，并在 5 分钟内提供 20% 的额外攻击速度、挖掘速度与攻击伤害。 （可通过配置文件调整）

Darker Depths is a underground expansion mod.

There are currently 3 biomes added to Darker Depths:

Glowshroom Forest

Spoiler The glowshroom cavern is where you will find large glowshrooms, the glowshroom monster, glowgrime, and the Glowshroom Cap. The Glowshroom Cap when worn, has limited durability, provides light, and also provides the user haste 1.

Sandy Catacombs

Spoiler The Sandy Catacombs is a desolate wasteland. Petrified roots hang from the ceiling, aridrock & boulders dot the inner cave landscape. A new class of mob without souls can be found here. They wander the catacombs in the hopes of binding with their next victim. Void Soul mobs when killed, don't die, but rather are turned into Void Souls. This orb is invulnerable, but will expire after 60 seconds. Upon right clicking with a glass bottle, Void Souls can be captured into a jar. Right clicking with Amber stops them from expiring. A new structure is found here, aptly named the Catacombs, where you will find Body Snatchers, Void Soul Knights, Tombs, Paranoia, and Forsaken Bronze. Cursed by the presence of the Void Souls, this Forsaken Bronze allows for unnatural abilities. This metal can be used to craft a few useful items, including the Death Anchor, the Stiletto, and Tombs.

Molten Cavern

Spoiler The Molten Cavern is a raging hot biome, where Deepslate has turned into Darkslate and lava can be found in layered pools. Geysers, Amber Clusters, and Dead Living Crystals can be found here. Dead Living Crystals can be reawakened by giving them a diamond. From there, they will start to grow back to life. When they have fully grown, they will turn into Living Crystal, which can spread to nearby diamond ore, or grow a taste for Melons, if you give them enough time. Crystal Melons are formed when a Living Crystal has a hunger for Melons. Placing a Melon near a living crystal will cause it to turn into a stone melon, then growing inside until it finally breaks free of its shell, converting to a Crystal Melon. Crystal Melons when used while in the offhand, will supercharge any tool to make it unbreakable, give 20% more attack speed, mining speed, and attack damage, for 5 minutes. (Adjustable via config)

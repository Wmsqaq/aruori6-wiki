A mod designed to encourage dietary variety!

It does so by rewarding the player for eating a variety of foods instead of punishing them for failing to diversify.
Instead of using the stick, we use the carrot!

官方介绍 （Modrinth · 中文版由 GLM AI 翻译，仅供参考）

由 Squeek502 制作的 The Spice Of Life 的非官方分支，并从 Cazsius 的 Spice of Life: Carrot Edition 向后移植了部分功能。

这个模组通过“收益递减”机制鼓励饮食多样化，并通过奖励尝试新食物来鼓励探索各种饮食。

功能：

  • 每吃下 50 点饥饿值的食物就获得一颗额外生命之心

  • 最近吃过的食物会触发收益递减

  • 工具提示会指示出尚未吃过的食物、已经吃过的食物，以及最近食用、会触发收益递减的食物

Unofficial Fork of The Spice Of Life by Squeek502 with features backported from Spice of Life: Carrot Edition by Cazsius

A mod that encourages dietary variety through diminishing returns, and dietary exploration by rewarding eating new foods.

Features:

  • Bonus heart for every 50 haunches of food eaten

  • Diminishing returns for food eaten recently

  • Tooltip indicator of foods that haven't been eaten yet, foods that have been eaten, and foods eaten recently contributing to diminishing returns

Credits

Thanks to Squeek502 for the original release of Spice of Life for 1.7.10, and to lordcazsius for the inspiration (and code from 1.12+) for the carrot edition!

Requires AppleCore Unofficial
